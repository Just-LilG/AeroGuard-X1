Risk Stages
Read Before Power-On
  • SIM800L power: never draw from the Uno's 5V pin. It needs a stable ~4V supply and can spike to 2A while calling — this will brown out the board mid-alert.
  • SIM800L RX line: the module runs at ~2.8V logic. Step the Uno's 5V TX signal down with a voltage divider before it reaches SIM800L RX.
  • Common ground: every part — sensors, LCD, servo, SD module, SIM800L, battery pack — shares one ground with the Arduino. No exceptions.
