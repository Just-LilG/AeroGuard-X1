AeroGuard-X1Assembly Manual — LPG Leak + Fire Early Warning

This is the kit you assemble. Wire the brain (Arduino Uno), gas nose, flame eye, screen, three lights, beeper, two buttons, and the SIM800L phone chip (real texts and calls). You do not have the SD card module — that is the only missing part. Leave D10–D13 empty. The contest phone app on Vercel is a pretend UI for judges. It does not talk to this box.

Read before power-on

  • You do not have the SD card module. Leave D10–D13 empty. That is the only missing part.
  • The phone chip (SIM800L) needs its own ~4V supply from the LM2596. Never feed it from the Uno 5V pin. Measure ~4.0V before SIM VCC.
  • Protect the chip’s listen pin: Uno D6 must go through resistors, never a bare 5V wire. You do not have a 20kΩ — use two 10kΩ in a line as 20k, or a 10k+10k pair (see the SIM step).
  • Join every ground. Uno, battery, 4V buck, SIM, sensors — one shared drain.
  • Put real phone numbers in the program before any live call or SMS demo.
  • Leave Uno pins A1 and A3 empty.

Build

Steps

Unplug USB while you move wires. Set 4.0V, then power the SIM800L. Skip the SD module — it is the only missing part.

01 — Power rails

5V for small parts · separate 4V for the phone chip · one GND

The Uno 5V pin is a tap for the nose, eye, screen, lights, beeper, and buttons. The SIM800L is thirsty. It needs its own fatter tap at about 4 volts from the LM2596 (a buck converter — a voltage reducer with a tiny screw).

  1. Red jumper: Uno 5V → breadboard + rail.
  2. Black jumper: Uno GND → breadboard − rail.
  3. LM2596 input from the USB power bank. Output will be ~4V for SIM VCC only.
  4. Join buck GND to the same − rail. Do not pour buck 4V into the Uno 5V pin.
Measure first. Power the buck. Turn the screw. Read about 4.0V on a multimeter before the SIM VCC wire ever goes on.

02 — Gas sensor

MQ-5 or MQ-2 · Uno A0 · 5V · GND

This module is the nose. A heater inside warms up. The first minutes can smell “hot electronics.” That is normal, like a kettle that smells of metal before the water boils.

Module pinGoes to
VCC5V rail
GNDGND rail
A0 / AO (analog)Uno A0
D0 / DO (digital)Leave unconnected

Clip it in the printed MQ mount later so air from the case grill can reach it. One zone for this kit.

03 — Flame sensor

KY-026 · analog to Uno A2

This is an eye for flame light, not a room thermometer. Aim the dark sensor toward where a flame would show through the case window.

Module pinGoes to
VCC5V
GNDGND
A0 analogUno A2
D0Leave unconnected
After the program is on the Uno, flick a lighter far from any gas. Watch the number in Serial Monitor (9600). If FIRE never trips, change FLAME_DETECT_THRESHOLD in the sketch a little.

04 — LCD screen

1602 I2C · A4 SDA · A5 SCL

A 16×2 character screen. The backpack on the back talks I2C — a two-wire chat. On the Uno those two wires are always A4 and A5.

LCD pinGoes to
VCC5V
GNDGND
SDAUno A4
SCLUno A5

Blank screen? Turn the contrast screw. Check the backlight jumper. Address is usually 0x27 or 0x3F. The program starts at 0x27.

05 — Status LEDs

D2 green · D3 yellow · D4 red · 220Ω each

Three stage lamps. Green = LOW (quiet early warning). Yellow = MEDIUM. Red = CRITICAL or FIRE.

  1. Bare 5mm LED: long leg is plus, short leg is minus.
  2. Plus → 220Ω resistor → Uno pin. Minus → GND. The resistor is a speed bump so the lamp does not burn.
  3. Green to D2. Yellow to D3. Red to D4.

If you bought a 3-pin LED module, follow S / + / − printed on that board. Signal still goes to D2 / D3 / D4.

06 — Buzzer

D8 · active beeper

The alarm. An active buzzer makes its own tone when the pin is driven.

  • + or S → Uno D8
  • − → GND

Three-pin modules: S to D8, − to GND, + to 5V only if the board asks for it. MEDIUM = short beeps. CRITICAL / FIRE = continuous.

07 — Buttons

D7 Reset · D9 Demo · other legs to GND

The program already uses an internal pull-up. The pin sits high until you press. Press ties it to ground (low). Do not wire these buttons to 5V.

ButtonOne legOther leg
Reset (mute, exit demo, recalibrate)D7GND
Demo (pretend leak stages)D9GND

Label the lid RESET and DEMO so you do not mix them in front of judges.

08 — SD module — skip today

Only missing part · leave D10–D13 empty

You do not have the SD board. That is the only part still to buy. Keep SD_ENABLED as false. The program still runs. Serial will say the SD is missing. That is OK.

When you buy it: CS → D10, MOSI → D11, MISO → D12, SCK → D13. Format the card FAT32. Then set SD_ENABLED to true and upload again. The file is gaslog.txt — a demo log, not a fire-proof vault.

09 — SIM800L phone chip — wire today

4V VCC · D5 TX · D6 via divider · antenna · nano-SIM

You have the mini phone. Screw the antenna on. Seat a nano-SIM with call + SMS credit (gold pads down, cut corner matching the drawing). Set 4.0V on the buck with the SIM VCC still disconnected. Then connect VCC. Keep GSM_ENABLED as true.

SIM800LGoes to
VCCBuck 4V out — never Uno 5V
GNDCommon GND
TXUno D5 (direct)
RXMid-point of the D6 divider

Divider — you do not have a 20kΩ. Two 10kΩ in a line equal 20k (use that to ground). One more 10k from D6 to the middle. If you only have two 10k total, use 10k up and 10k down (~2.5V). SIM RX comes off the middle. Never a bare D6 wire.

Uno D6  --- 10kΩ ---+--- SIM800L RX
                    |
              10k+10k (or one 10k)
                    |
                   GND

Many boards blink fast while hunting, then slow blink when they have joined the network.

Demo caution: CRITICAL and FIRE place a real call, then a real SMS. Warn the person who holds the owner phone. Put your own number in if you are testing alone.

10 — Case

126 × 90 × 36 mm · OpenSCAD → STL

Open aeroguard_x1_case.scad in OpenSCAD (free 3D-shape software). Leave part = "all_export". Press F6. Export STL. Print base, lid, and the MQ sensor clip (PLA or PETG, about 0.2 mm layers).

  1. Seat the Uno on the standoffs. USB faces the side hole.
  2. SIM and 4V buck on their platforms. Leave the SD platform empty.
  3. Leave the extra empty shelf empty.
  4. LCD in the lid window. LEDs in G / Y / R. Buttons in DEMO and RESET. Buzzer in its hole.
  5. Gas sensor in the MQ clip at the grill. Flame sensor toward the side window.
  6. Antenna out the front slot. Four lid screws.

11 — Phone numbers and upload

aeroguard_x1-1.ino · Arduino IDE · USB data cable · Serial 9600

You load one program: aeroguard_x1-1.ino onto the Arduino Uno.

  1. Install Arduino IDE (the desktop app from Arduino).
  2. Plug a data USB cable (charge-only cables look the same and will not upload).
  3. Tools → Board → Arduino Uno. Tools → Port → the port that appears with the cable in.
  4. Library Manager: install LiquidCrystal I2C. SD and SoftwareSerial are already built in.
  5. Set OWNER_CONTACT and SECONDARY_CONTACT like +23324…. Set DEVICE_LABEL to the place name.
  6. Keep GSM_ENABLED as true. Keep SD_ENABLED as false.
  7. Click Upload. Open Serial Monitor at 9600.
LCD shows a filling bar for about 5 seconds (Sniffing air...). That is the box learning quiet air. Press Demo during the bar if you want to skip it.
