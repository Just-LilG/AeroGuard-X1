Risk Stages
Read Before Power-On
  • SIM800L power: never draw from the Uno's 5V pin. It needs a stable ~4V supply and can spike to 2A while calling — this will brown out the board mid-alert.
  • SIM800L RX line: the module runs at ~2.8V logic. Step the Uno's 5V TX signal down with a voltage divider before it reaches SIM800L RX.
  • Common ground: every part — sensors, LCD, servo, SD module, SIM800L, battery pack — shares one ground with the Arduino. No exceptions.

Power Rails
5VGND

Set up your breadboard with a 5V rail and a GND rail, both fed from the Arduino's 5V and GND pins.

Keep the battery pack and SIM800L's regulated 4V supply on a separate section, tied back only by a shared ground wire.

02
Gas Sensors — Zone 1 & 2
A0A15VGND

VCC to 5V, GND to ground rail, analog output (AOUT) to A0 (Zone 1) and A1 (Zone 2).

Mount each sensor in its intended zone. MQ-series sensors need a few minutes' warm-up on first power — normal behavior, not a fault.

03
Flame Sensor
A25VGND

VCC to 5V, GND to ground, analog output to A2.

Calibrate before final assembly — trigger briefly and safely with a lighter, away from any gas source, and confirm the reading in Serial Monitor.
04
LCD Display (I2C)
A4 SDAA5 SCL

VCC to 5V, GND to ground, SDA to A4, SCL to A5.

Blank screen once running? Run an I2C scanner sketch to confirm the address — commonly 0x27 or 0x3F.

05
Status LEDs
D2D3D4

Yellow→D2, Orange→D3, Red→D4. Long leg (anode) through a 220Ω resistor to its pin, short leg (cathode) to ground.

06
Buzzer
D8

Positive lead to D8, negative lead to ground.

07
Reset / Recalibrate Button
D7

One leg to D7, other leg to ground. Code uses the Uno's internal pull-up — no external resistor needed.

08
Vent Servo
D95VGND

Brown/black to ground, red to 5V, signal (orange/yellow) to D9. Mount so the arm can physically open the vent flap.

09
SD Card Logging Module
D10 CSD11D12D13

VCC to 5V, GND to ground, CS to D10, MOSI to D11, MISO to D12, SCK to D13.

Fixed pins — D11–D13 are claimed automatically by the SD library. Nothing else goes there.
10
SIM800L GSM Module
D5 RXD6 TX4V (separate)

VCC to regulated ~4V — never the Uno's 5V pin. TX to D5. RX to D6 through a voltage divider.

Power warning — calling/SMS spikes to ~2A. A shared, unregulated supply will brown out the Uno mid-alert.
11
Battery Backup
5V parallelGND

Wire the battery pack's regulated output in parallel with your main 5V supply, ground shared with everything else. Keeps the system alive through a power outage.
