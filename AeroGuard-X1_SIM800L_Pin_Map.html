Where each used hole goes

The four wires

HoleGoes toHow
VCCCell plus18650 bump, or phone-battery +. Never Uno 5V or 3.3V.
GNDCell minus + Uno GNDStraight wire. No resistor.

If the light died

The GND resistor is starving the chip

The 10k to ground is only a tiny side pipe on the RXD talk wire. It is not part of the power drain. If you put that resistor in the SIM GND hole as the only ground, the pump cannot drink. The light goes off. No blink. No text. No call.

Wrong — light dies

Resistor sitting in the fat drain.

Cell minus --- 10kΩ --- SIM GND     ← never
Uno GND    --- 10kΩ --- SIM GND     ← never
Cell plus  --- 10kΩ --- SIM VCC     ← never

Right — light can blink

Fat drain is a straight black wire. Resistors only touch D6 / RXD.

Cell minus -------- SIM GND
Uno GND    -------- SIM GND
                    (no resistor)

Uno D6 --- 10k ---+--- SIM RXD
                  |
                10k
                  |
              GND rail

Do this in order. Do not skip.

  1. Unplug both 10k resistors. Unplug D5 and D6 too. Leave RST empty.
  2. Only two power wires, both straight: cell plus → SIM VCC. Cell minus + Uno GND → SIM GND.
  3. Antenna on. SIM seated. The light should come back: fast blink, then maybe slow blink. If it stays dark, the cell is empty, backwards, or VCC/GND are swapped.
  4. Then add TXD → D5 straight. No resistor.
  5. Last: the quiet-down pair on RXD only. One 10k from Uno D6 to SIM RXD. One 10k from that same RXD joint to the breadboard GND rail (the fat drain), not as the only wire in the SIM GND hole.
When the light blinks again, Demo twice to MEDIUM. The screen should say texting owner (no wait-network pause).

Leave these closed

Empty holes

  • NET — not the antenna. Leave empty.
  • RST — leave empty. Unplug any wire here.
  • RING, DTR, MIC, SPK — no mic, no speaker. The box texts and calls by itself.

Slide the nano-SIM into the silver tray. Gold pads toward the board. Cut corner matches. Network light: fast blink = hunting. Slow blink = on the network.
